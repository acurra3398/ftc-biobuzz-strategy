# Third-party material

**`assets/biobuzz.webp`** — the BIOBUZZ field image, taken from the
[Pedro Pathing Visualizer](https://github.com/manaflow-ai/pedro-pathing-visualizer)
(`public/fields/biobuzz.webp`), which is licensed Apache 2.0. Used here as the
background of the field view so positions can be checked against the real
layout. Apache 2.0 requires that this attribution travel with the file.

**Game data** — field dimensions, point values and ranking-point thresholds in
`js/defaults.js` are transcribed from *BIOBUZZ presented by RTX, Competition
Manual V1* (© FIRST). The manual itself is deliberately not committed; download
it from [firstinspires.org](https://www.firstinspires.org/resource-library).

**Coordinate frame** — the 141.5 in field interior and the origin-bottom-left,
+y-up convention match the Pedro Pathing Visualizer, so coordinates are
interchangeable between the two tools.
